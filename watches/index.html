Watches & sensors

Your instruments, joined up.

What works with XC Core today, and what is still being built. We only call something ready once it has been tested on real hardware.

External Bluetooth varios

In the app now, in testing

XC Core can use a Bluetooth flight instrument's sensors instead of the phone's: Settings → General → Sensors → External vario. It searches, connects, remembers the instrument and reconnects by itself.

  • Bluetooth Low Energy: the Stodeus UltraBip first, with profiles for XC Tracer, BlueFly and Flymaster.
  • Classic Bluetooth (Android): instruments paired in the phone's Bluetooth settings.
  • Data: LK8EX1, LXWP0, BlueFly, XC Tracer and NMEA position sentences.
  • Battery saving: while the instrument's pressure is used, the phone's barometer and motion sensors rest; with the instrument's GPS, the phone's GPS rests too.
  • Fallback: if the instrument stops or looks wrong, XC Core says "Sensor lost, using phone" and carries on with no jump in altitude or climb.
  • The instrument is named in the IGC file.

This is built and checked with recorded data; its first test with a real UltraBip is coming up. Other makes count as supported only once they've been tested. If you fly with one, tell us how it works.

Watches

No XC Core watch app can be installed yet. They are being built and tested; this is where each one stands.

  • Wear OS

    In testing

    The watch app is built and runs on its own on a watch emulator. The link to the phone is in the Google Play version of XC Core, waiting for a test with a real watch before the watch app goes on Google Play.

  • Huawei

    Waiting for Huawei

    Written for both kinds of Huawei watch (the GT, Fit and D2 series, and the HarmonyOS watches). Waiting for Huawei's permission to link the watch to the phone, then a test on a watch.

  • Garmin

    In development

    A Connect IQ app is written; not yet built or tested.

  • Apple Watch

    Later

    After the iPhone version.

  • Amazfit (Zepp OS)

    Planned

    Not started.

  • Coros, Suunto, Polar, Fitbit

    Not possible

    These watches can't run this kind of app for other developers.

What the watch apps are designed to do

Once they're ready, the watch adjusts to its own sensors:

  • Full vario: a watch with a barometer and a speaker gives a climb rate and beeps.
  • Silent vario: a barometer but no speaker, so climb is felt as vibration.
  • Display: no barometer, so the watch shows the numbers, with a slower climb rate from GPS.

With the phone, the watch shows your climb, altitude, height, speed and flight time, and buzzes with the phone's warnings. On its own, it can record your track for the flight log. These are design goals until the watch apps are released.

Phones

  • Android 7.0 or later, with GPS. A barometer is strongly recommended; without one the vario works on GPS alone, more slowly.
  • Huawei phones without Google services: a version for AppGallery is being prepared.
  • iPhone: coming. It isn't on the App Store or TestFlight yet.
