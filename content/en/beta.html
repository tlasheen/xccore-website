How to join (Android)

  1. Use your Android phone, signed in to Google Play with the Google account you want to test with.
  2. {{group_en}}Join the testers' Google Group. The group's link is coming soon. Until then, email {{email}} with the address of that Google account, and we'll add you.{{/group_en}}
  3. Open the test's opt-in page and tap Become a tester: {{opt_in}}
  4. Install XC Core from Google Play. It can take a few minutes to appear after you opt in: {{store}}
  5. Keep the app installed and open it now and then for at least 14 days: Google asks for new apps to be tested that long before they're released to everyone.
  6. Tell us what you find, with Send private feedback on the app's Google Play page or by email to {{email}}. Your phone's model helps.

The app and its voice are in English. Huawei phones without Google services can't join through Google Play; an AppGallery version is being prepared. The iPhone version is coming.
