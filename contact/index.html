Contact & support

Talk to the pilot who builds it.

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to tlasheen@gmail.com. XC Core is made by one pilot-developer, so replies can take a day or two.

Reporting a problem

Tell us your phone's model and Android version, the flight mode, and what happened. If it's about a flight, you can attach its IGC file from the flight log (only if you want to: it contains your track).

Questions

Is XC Core free?

Yes. No ads and no purchases. XC mode is free for a limited time.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 or later with GPS. A barometer is strongly recommended; without one the vario works on GPS alone, more slowly. An iPhone version is coming, and a version for Huawei phones without Google services is being prepared for AppGallery.

Does it work without a phone signal?

Yes. The vario, voice coach, 3D view and recording need no internet. Download your area's map before you go, and terrain downloads by itself where you are.

Does it work with the screen off?

Yes. The vario, voice, GPS and recording keep running with the screen off or the phone locked. Some phones close apps in the background to save battery: the app helps you allow it to keep running.

Can I send my flights to XContest?

Yes, you can claim flights on XContest straight from the app: log in once, then tap Claim on the flight. Or share the IGC file. XC Core's files aren't signed yet (no G record), so XContest accepts them but scores them at 0 points for now.

Can I use my watch or Bluetooth vario?

Bluetooth varios: yes, in testing, starting with the Stodeus UltraBip. Watches: not yet. See watches and sensors.

Which languages?

The app and its voice are in English for now.

What happens to my data?

Nothing is sent to us: there are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Your flights and settings stay on your phone. Map and terrain providers see which area is downloaded, and XContest gets a flight only when you claim it. Read the privacy policy.

Can I rely on it like a certified instrument?

No. XC Core is a flight aid, not a certified instrument. Its readings and suggestions can be wrong: keep watch outside, fly within your skills and make your own decisions about terrain, airspace, weather and landing.
