XC Core

Hear where the lift is.

A paragliding and paramotor vario for your Android phone, with a voice coach that talks you into the thermal core so your eyes stay outside.

Join the beta iPhone: coming soon

XC Core turns your phone into a flight instrument for paragliding, speed flying and paramotoring. Its barometer, GPS and motion sensors are combined into a fast, smooth climb rate. As soon as you circle, XC Core maps the lift around you and tells you what to do: tighten, open up, straighten now, or hold it. It's free, with no ads and no account, and it's in beta on Google Play now.

Four ways to fly

Choose a mode when you open the app. Each has its own screen, sounds and layout.

  • Speed

    Big height above ground, speed and heading, with a low-height alarm and a turbulence bar. No map to distract you.

  • Soaring

    A live lift line on the map: where you found lift and sink on your earlier passes, kept for each site.

  • XC

    A map while gliding, a 3D thermal view when you circle, the voice coach, and a compass pointing to the best thermal you can reach.

  • PPG

    Five large readings for paramotor pilots, with a climb rate that stays steady through engine vibration.

In the air

Private by design

No ads, no account, no analytics, no tracking. Your flights, profile and settings stay on your phone; nothing is sent to us. The map and terrain providers see which area is downloaded, and XContest gets a flight only when you claim it. The details are in the privacy policy.

Join the beta

XC Core is being tested by pilots on Google Play. Join the test group, install it, fly it and tell us what works and what doesn't.

How to join بالعربي

XC Core is a flight aid, not a certified instrument. Its readings and suggestions can be wrong: keep watch outside, fly within your skills and make your own decisions.
