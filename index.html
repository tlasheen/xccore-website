How it sounds

A coach in your ear, not a screen to stare at.

The beeps climb with the lift. As soon as you circle, the voice tells you what to do, at the moment to do it.

  1. On a glide, lift on your right+0.8 m/s
    “Thermal here, turn right.”
  2. Circling, the core is inside+1.6 m/s
    “Tighten.”
  3. In the core+2.7 m/s
    “Centred, hold it.”

In the air

Every screen made for flying.

Four ways to fly

Choose a mode. Get the screen for it.

Each mode has its own layout, sounds and voice. Arrange the widgets your way on the ground.

  • Speed

    Big height above ground, speed and heading. A low-height alarm. No map.

  • Soaring

    A live lift line on the map, from your earlier passes along the ridge.

  • XC

    Map on the glide, 3D view in the climb, the voice coach and a thermal compass.

  • PPG

    Five big readings, a climb rate steady through engine vibration.

Land. Claim it on XContest.

Claim your flight on XContest straight from the app, no computer needed. Log in once, tap Claim, done.

Every flight is saved as a full IGC file, one fix a second, with takeoff and landing found by themselves. Share it, export KML for Google Earth, or replay it through the whole app with sound.

More about logging

Fly the route.

Type, paste or drop waypoints. The voice counts you in from 100 metres, then points to the next.

After landing, a route badge with your times, ready to share. One switch hides positions and the map.

More about routes

  • Screen off, still flying

    Vario, voice, GPS and recording keep running with the phone locked in your pocket.

  • Works with no signal

    Offline topo maps and terrain. The coach and the 3D view need no internet.

  • Private by design

    No ads, no account, no tracking. Your flights stay on your phone. Privacy policy
